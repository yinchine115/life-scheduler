# LifeScheduler

A lightweight, offline life scheduling assistant for Windows.

## Features

- **Offline by design** — no internet connection required after activation
- **Lifetime license** — one-time payment, no subscription
- **Zero admin rights** — runs from your user directory
- **Machine-code activation** — your license stays with your device

After purchase, send your **machine code** (found in the app's *About* dialog) along with your **order number** to **yinchine2019@gmail.com**.

You will receive your registration code within 24 hours.
