Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. No Data Collection

Life-Scheduler is a fully offline software for Windows 10/11. After activation, it requires no internet connection. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal data.

2. Local Data Only

All task schedules, countdowns, reminders, and settings are stored locally on your device. Your data never leaves your computer.

3. License Activation

License verification uses RSA-signed offline verification. Activation links your machine code with your License Key locally. No personal usage data is sent to our servers.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Creem.io. We do not handle or store your credit card information. Please refer to Creem's privacy policy for payment data handling.
