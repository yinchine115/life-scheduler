Privacy Policy

Life-Scheduler · Lightweight offline task scheduler for Windows

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Overview

Life-Scheduler is a fully offline, privacy-first task scheduler for Windows 10/11. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. In short: Life-Scheduler does not collect, transmit, or phone home any personal data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Because the application runs entirely offline, Life-Scheduler does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details;
  • Your files, schedules, tasks, or reminders;
  • Your browsing history, location, or usage analytics;
  • Any telemetry, crash reports, or device identifiers.

No data leaves your computer when you use the software.

Information Collected During Purchase

When you purchase a license through our payment processor (Creem), the following may be collected by that third party to complete the transaction: your name, email address, billing information, and payment details. We do not store or have direct access to your full payment information. Please refer to Creem's Privacy Policy for details.

License & Activation

License activation uses an offline RSA-signed key. The activation process verifies the license locally on your machine and does not require an internet connection or account registration.

Children's Privacy

Life-Scheduler is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
